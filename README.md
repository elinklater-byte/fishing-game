# 🎣 Hook, Line & Sinker

A browser fishing game. Start on the dock with 100 coins, buy a Tiny Raft and a Rusty Rod, and head out to sea.

- Walk to the **left side** of your boat, click the water, and steer your lure into fish with the mouse (or your finger).
- Your slack (line length) limits how far the lure can go. Better fish live deeper.
- Strong fish take several hits before they're hooked.
- The ocean is 190 m deep and gets darker as you go. Dive straight down fast and fish can't bite, which gets you past the small ones.
- Some trips have a rare **shipwreck** on the sea floor, with a crowd of deep fish and the wreck-only Treasure Fish.
- Your bucket holds 4 fish to start. Walk to the **right side** of the boat to fillet them: drag the knife across each fish at the right speed (keep the needle in the green zone).
- Sail back to the dock, sell your fillets at the market, and upgrade your rod, lure, slack, knife, bucket and boat.

Controls: ◀ ▶ / A D / arrow keys to walk · click water to cast · move mouse to steer · Space to reel in · F to fillet.

Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Progress saves in your browser.
