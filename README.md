# 🎣 Hook, Line & Sinker

A browser fishing game. Start on the dock with 100 coins, buy a Tiny Raft and a Rusty Rod, and head out to sea.

- Walk to the **left side** of your boat, click the water, and steer your lure into fish with the mouse (or your finger).
- Your slack (line length) limits how far the lure can go. Deeper fish are worth more.
- Strong fish take several hits before they're hooked. They keep swimming the same way when hit.
- Herring, mackerel, striped bass, tuna and amberjack swim in **schools**.
- All 16 fish are real ocean species, from Atlantic herring to the coelacanth. A **fish fact** shows in the bottom right while you're at sea.
- The ocean is 250 m deep and gets darker as you go. Dive straight down fast and fish can't bite, which gets you past the small ones.
- Some trips have a rare **shipwreck** on the sea floor, home to the Goliath Grouper.
- Walk to the **right side** of the boat to fillet: drag the knife across each fish. Cuts in the green speed zone are clean; too fast or too slow and the fish bleeds, and each messy cut lowers its value by 20%.
- Sail back to the dock, sell your fillets, and upgrade your rod, lure, slack, knife and bucket.

## Boats

There are 7 boats: Tiny Raft, Rowboat, Skiff, Fishing Boat, Trawler, Research Vessel and Deep-Sea Explorer. You can only buy the next boat once all five gear upgrades are maxed (Lv 4). The new boat unlocks new fish and resets your gear to Lv 1 of a **stronger set** (e.g. Rusty Rod → Bamboo Rod).

Controls: ◀ ▶ / A D / arrow keys to walk · click water to cast · move mouse to steer · Space to reel in · F to fillet.

Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Progress saves in your browser.
